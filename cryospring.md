# CryoSpring 冷泉 — Immersion Liquid Cooling

Immersion liquid cooling technology that fully submerges heat sources in
coolant, using phase change to dissipate heat for ultimate thermal performance.

## About CryoSpring

Developed by LIGHTNINGASIC through years of solving Bitcoin mining heat
dissipation needs. By fully submerging heat sources in coolant and using
phase-change to carry away heat.

## Key Advantages

- Significantly reduced chip operating temperatures.
- Near-silent operation.
- Completely dust-free environment.
- Substantially extended equipment lifespan.
- Supports overclocking, reducing per-unit hashrate cost.
