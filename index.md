# LIGHTNINGASIC

Making AI & Blockchain accessible to everyone. Hong Kong-based Bitcoin and
cryptocurrency mining hardware company, founded in 2013 by JACK LIAO (founder
of Bitcoin Gold / BTG).

## About

- **Innovation Pioneer** — Launched the world's first Bitcoin/Litecoin dual mining cluster in 2013, and released BitExchange ATM & hardware wallet in 2014.
- **Cooling Technology** — Developed CryoSpring immersion liquid cooling technology based on Bitcoin mining heat dissipation needs.
- **Secure Wallet** — Developed HardID hardware wallet — fully open-source, air-gapped, dual EAL6+ secure elements, Clear Sign.
- **AI Era** — In 2026, partnering with ETHERCOIN.com and ICLAWMINI.com to launch thin clients for the AI TOKEN ERA.

## History

- **2013** — World's First Dual Mining Cluster (Bitcoin & Litecoin dual-algorithm).
